Estimate assumptions

The 5/5 rate is initially fixed for 5 years, the 7/1 for 7 years, and the 10/1 for 10 years. First mortgage payments can change after those periods. Future adjustments are not modeled.

Home equity payments assume full amortization over 180 months at the entered fixed interest rate. First mortgage payments use 360 months.

No mortgage insurance, closing costs, prepaid expenses, or cash-to-close calculation is included. Financing 100% of the price does not mean zero cash is due at closing.

The supplied home equity maximum is $250,000. This calculator checks that amount only; it does not establish product availability, credit qualification, property eligibility, CLTV eligibility, or approval.
